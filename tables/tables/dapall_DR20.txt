## Description

The file `dapall_DR20.fits` is a value-added catalog produced from the analysis of LVM DR20 data using a Pipe3D-like Data Analysis Pipeline (DAP), in which each row corresponds to an individual exposure (identified by `tileid`, `mjd`, and `expnum`) and contains integrated physical properties derived from the stellar continuum fitting and emission-line measurements of the observed spectra; the table combines basic observational metadata (position and exposure information) with stellar population parameters (effective temperature, surface gravity, metallicity, alpha-enhancement, extinction, velocity dispersion, systemic velocity, and fit quality metrics) and flux measurements of key emission lines across the optical and near-infrared range (including Balmer lines, [O II], [O III], [N II], [S II], and [S III]), together with their associated uncertainties, providing a compact representation of both stellar and ionized gas properties suitable for statistical analysis, source classification, and quick-look science without requiring direct access to the full spectral data products.

## Notes

- This table represents **average spectral properties per exposure**.
- Emission-line fluxes correspond to **parametric fits (pek)**.
- Suitable for:
  - emission-line diagnostics (e.g., BPT diagrams)
  - stellar population studies
  - quick-look science analysis
